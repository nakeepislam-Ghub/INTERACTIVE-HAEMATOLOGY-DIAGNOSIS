# Interactive Haematology Diagnosis

A free, offline, interactive haematology book for medical students, haematology residents, and Master's and MD candidates.

**Open the book:** https://nakeepislam-ghub.github.io/INTERACTIVE-HAEMATOLOGY-DIAGNOSIS/

## Authors

- **Prof. Abeer Mohammed, MD PhD**, Professor of Clinical Pathology, Faculty of Medicine for Girls, Al-Azhar University: scientific content.
- **Ass. Prof. Islam Fathy Elnakeeb**, Assistant Professor of Clinical Pathology, Faculty of Medicine, Aswan University: concept, design, editing and the interactive edition (built with AI assistance).

The authors offer this book free for public benefit.

## What is inside

- 43 lecture chapters from Prof. Abeer Mohammed's notes: red cells, spleen and marrow failure, myeloid and lymphoid neoplasms, flow cytometry, haemostasis and thrombosis, white cell and histiocytic disorders, laboratory haematology.
- A supplementary Foundations part (F1 to F7) on normal blood and marrow, written from standard textbooks.
- Narrated 3D animations, animated diagrams, yes/no work-up charts, calculators, cases and quizzes.
- A haematology atlas with an Identify quiz, and a Practice page with flashcards, questions, cases, algorithms and checklists.

## Install it on your device

1. Open the link above in Chrome or Edge (Safari on iPhone and iPad).
2. Computer: press the Install icon in the address bar. Phone: choose "Add to Home screen".
3. The book then opens like an app and works without internet. It updates itself the next time you open it while online.

Lecture videos stream from YouTube and need an internet connection.
